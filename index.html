Our space

Sit under the Kennesaw mural.

Navy counters, honey-toned wood, woven pendant lights and a wall-sized painting of the town. Booths for families, tables for regulars, and roll-up glass doors that open to the patio when the weather agrees.

Room for everyone.

Bring the whole table. For larger groups, call or email ahead and the team will help you plan.
